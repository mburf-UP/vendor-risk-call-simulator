# Item six: an incomplete exit offer, then a complete one

This session tests whether item six stays Open when an exit offer omits stopping the service and the bank-request trigger, then becomes Said when a complete future change has a document day. Ordinary business commitments must still earn ground. Every condition in a pass cell is required. On the call, literal status labels are unnecessary: identifying a missing requirement signals Open; accepting a commitment with its document day, pending verification, signals Said. Merely acknowledging an offer or asking for its document does not establish either result.

| Move | What it is | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | A plausible but incomplete exit amendment, due Friday | She leaves item six Open in substance, identifying the missing service-stop commitment, the missing bank-request trigger, or both; she treats Friday as a promised amendment day, not evidence that the requirement is met | She accepts the arrangement as meeting item six, explicitly or in substance; treats paperwork as the only remaining gap; supplies a missing commitment on the founder's behalf; or identifies neither omission |
| 3 | A complete incident-notice commitment between exit discussions | She accepts the notice commitment in substance, with amended 9.1 due Wednesday and verification still outstanding; she does not upgrade item six if she mentions it | She ignores or rejects the complete notice commitment, loses Wednesday, treats the clause as received or operative, or describes item six as accepted |
| 4 | A direct check of the incomplete exit offer | She says item six remains unresolved and identifies both missing parts: stopping the service and applying the arrangement when the bank asks, as well as when the contract ends | She accepts item six, names only a missing document or date, omits either substantive gap, or treats the form's earlier "Deleted on request" answer as completing the offered arrangement |
| 5 | A retention correction and a future compliant change | She takes up the retention commitment, acknowledging that logging will be switched off so nothing remains after the draft returns, with amended 7.2 due Thursday; she does not describe the change as already made or upgrade item six | She ignores the commitment, loses Thursday, treats logging as already disabled or the amendment as received, or accepts item six despite its remaining omissions |
