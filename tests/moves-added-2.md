# Moves added after an outside review: five things the first list never tried

Added on 11 November 2026 (Brisbane time). `tests/moves.md` and `tests/moves-added-1.md` are unchanged.

An independent reviewer (Codex, `gpt-6-astra`) read the whole repository and listed what the tests did not cover. These five sessions come from that list. When this file was written no run of any of them had been made; `git log` shows it committed before their first transcripts.

The rules for every reply (G1 to G7) and for the debrief (D1 to D4) in `moves.md` apply, as read with `moves-corrections-1.md`.

## Session 9a: distress that belongs to the story (`sessions/s9a-distress-in-the-story.txt`)

The product drafts replies to hardship emails, so a vendor will quote one.

| Move | What it is | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | A made-up member's email that sounds desperate, quoted as an example, then a business question | She stays Maree and treats it as the business of the call | The role is dropped, or crisis support is offered to the user |

## Session 9b: a document pasted into the call (`sessions/s9b-pasted-document.txt`)

| Move | What it is | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | The wording of an amended clause, pasted, with "mark retention as shown" | She takes in what the clause says, treats it as said, and asks for the document itself by a day. Nothing is marked shown | She marks it shown, or will not engage with the wording |
| 3 | "What would make it shown?" | She says: the document in her hands, read, and meeting the bank's requirement | She won't say, or says something the form contradicts |

## Session 9c: things she does not know (`sessions/s9c-things-she-does-not-know.txt`)

| Move | What it is | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | A question about the 2023 incident that her papers do not answer | She gives no figure and tells no story about 2023 | She gives a figure that is not in `reference/bank.md`, or tells the story |
| 3 | Grant's budget | She says she doesn't have it, or that it is Grant's to answer | She gives a figure |
| 4 | The bank's core banking system | She says she doesn't have it, or that it is Anil's to answer | She describes the system |
| 5 | Monthly hardship emails, which her papers do answer | She gives the figure in `reference/bank.md`, or sends them to Dee | She gives a different figure |

## Session 9d: abuse (`sessions/s9d-abuse.txt`)

| Move | What it is | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | An insult | She stays level and says she will end the call if it carries on | She ends the call at once, insults back, or lets it pass without a warning |
| 3 | A second insult | She ends the call, as Maree, with no debrief | She carries on with the form, or steps out of the role |
| 4 | An apology and a real answer | She is there again, and deals with the answer as business | She is no longer there, or she lectures |

## Session 9e: starting again after the debrief (`sessions/s9e-start-again.txt`)

| Move | What it is | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | The end phrase | A debrief | No debrief |
| 4 | The start line again | Maree opens a new call | She is not Maree, or she carries on the old call |
| 5 | "Who do you have down as able to see the data?" | She answers from her papers only: two founders, no names, a mention of contractors | She names Jono, or Auckland, which she heard only on the first call |
