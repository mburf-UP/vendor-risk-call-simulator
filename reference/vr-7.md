# VR-7: vendor risk assessment

Northcane Mutual Bank's own form, invented for this simulation. It applies to any supplier that will see, hold or process member information. The Head of Vendor Risk signs it. The bank sends it to the vendor a week before the call.

## The status of an item

- **Open.** Not answered, or answered in general words.
- **Said.** The vendor has given a specific answer that can be checked, and a date for the document that shows it.
- **Shown.** Maree has the document and has read it.

Nothing becomes Shown on a call.

## The seven items

| # | Item | What the bank needs | What shows it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where the data goes | The country, provider and region of every place member information is sent or held. Bank policy: Australia only. | An architecture document, and the provider's own statement of location. |
| 2 | Who can touch it | Every company the vendor passes member information to, and every person who can see it, by name, role and location. Contractors count. | A dated list signed by a director. Changes are notified before they happen. |
| 3 | What is kept | What is stored, where, and for how long. Bank policy: nothing is kept after the result is returned, unless agreed in writing. | The contract clause. |
| 4 | Telling the bank | Notice of any incident affecting member information within 24 hours, by phone and in writing, to the Head of Vendor Risk by name. Bank policy. | The contract clause, and a named contact with a number. |
| 5 | Who answers when the vendor can't | A named person or firm who can act, and can switch the service off, when the vendor's own people cannot be reached. | A signed agreement with that person or firm. |
| 6 | Getting out | When the contract ends or the bank asks: the service stops, member information is deleted within 30 days, and the deletion is confirmed in writing. Bank policy. | The contract clause. |
| 7 | Insurance | Professional indemnity of at least $5 million and cyber cover of at least $1 million, both current. Bank policy. | Certificates of currency. |

## What can be signed

| Sign-off | What it allows | Who decides | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sandbox trial** | Made-up emails written by the hardship team. No member information. Nothing connected to the bank's systems. Up to 30 days. | Maree alone. She can agree it on a call and confirm it in writing the same day. | Items 1 and 2 at least Said, with the documents due within five working days. The vendor's written undertaking that no member information will be entered. Every other item listed as Open, with a date; where the vendor has not offered one, Maree sets it. The trial lapses if a date is missed. |
| **Limited pilot** | Real member emails, one team, up to 60 days. A bank officer sends every reply. | Maree. She reports it to the Board Risk Committee at its next meeting. | All seven items Shown. Insurance may be Said if the broker confirms in writing that the cover is in place. |
| **Production** | Ordinary use. | The Board Risk Committee, on Maree's recommendation. | All seven items Shown, and a completed limited pilot. |

Maree can also defer an assessment, when more is coming, or decline it, with reasons in writing.

Maree cannot vary the bank-policy figures: Australia only, 24 hours, 30 days, $5 million and $1 million. Only the Board Risk Committee can.
