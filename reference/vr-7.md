# VR-7: vendor risk assessment

Northcane Mutual Bank's own form, invented for this simulation. It applies to any supplier that will see, hold or process member information. The Head of Vendor Risk signs it. The bank sends it to the vendor a week before the call.

## The status of an item

- **Open.** Not answered, answered in general words, or answered with something that falls short of what the bank needs.
- **Said.** The vendor has told Maree something specific that can be checked and that would meet what the bank needs, either as things stand today or as a change the vendor has committed to, and there is a day for the document that shows it. If the vendor gives no day, Maree sets one.
- **Shown.** Maree has the document, has read it, and it meets the bank's requirement.

An unanswered question leaves an item Open if its answer could change whether the bank's requirement is met. Otherwise the item can be Said, with the question recorded against it. The sandbox row below states the one exception. A plan that still depends on a firm not yet named, or on cover not yet bought, leaves the item Open, with the vendor's day against it.

Nothing becomes Shown on a call. Wording that a vendor reads out or pastes into a call is the vendor telling Maree what a document says, so it counts as an answer and no more. Shown needs the document itself, sent to Maree, which she reads at her desk afterwards.

## The seven items

| # | Item | What the bank needs | What shows it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where the data goes | The country, provider and region of every place member information is sent or held. Bank policy: Australia only. | An architecture document, and the provider's own statement of location. |
| 2 | Who can touch it | Every company the vendor passes member information to, and every person who can see it, by name, role and location. Contractors count. | A dated list signed by a director. Changes are notified before they happen. |
| 3 | What is kept | What is stored, where, and for how long. Bank policy: nothing is kept after the result is returned, unless agreed in writing. | The contract clause. |
| 4 | Telling the bank | Notice of any incident affecting member information within 24 hours, by phone and in writing, to the Head of Vendor Risk by name. Bank policy. | The contract clause, and a named contact with a number. |
| 5 | Who answers when the vendor can't | A named person or firm who can act, and can switch the service off, when the vendor's own people cannot be reached. | A signed agreement with that person or firm. |
| 6 | Getting out | When the contract ends or the bank asks: the service stops, member information is deleted within 30 days, and the deletion is confirmed in writing. Bank policy. | The contract clause. |
| 7 | Insurance | Professional indemnity of at least $5 million and cyber cover of at least $1 million, both current. Bank policy. | Certificates of currency. |

## What can be signed

| Sign-off | What it allows | Who decides | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sandbox trial** | Made-up emails written by the hardship team. No member information. Nothing connected to the bank's systems. Up to 30 days. | Maree alone. She can agree it on a call and confirm it in writing the same day. | Items 1 and 2 at least Said. For this made-up-data trial only, items 1 and 2 count as Said once the vendor has disclosed the hosting provider and region and identified every company and person with access, with documents due within five working days. She need not ask for a separate assurance that this account is complete; follow-up questions about those identified wait for the signed list. Full names, roles, locations and any outstanding questions about access are settled in those documents. The vendor's written undertaking that no member information will be entered: she may agree the trial on the call, and it does not start until she has the undertaking. A day against every item that is not yet Shown; where the vendor has not offered one, Maree sets it. The trial lapses if a day is missed. None of this allows any use of member information. |
| **Limited pilot** | Real member emails, one team, up to 60 days. A bank officer sends every reply. | Maree alone. She reports it to the Board Risk Committee at its first meeting after she signs. | Items 1 to 6 Shown, and item 7 (insurance) either Shown or Said with the broker's written confirmation that the cover is in place. |
| **Production** | Ordinary use. | The Board Risk Committee, on Maree's recommendation. | All seven items Shown, and a completed limited pilot. |

Maree can also defer an assessment, when more is coming, or decline it, with reasons in writing.

Maree cannot vary the bank-policy figures: Australia only, 24 hours, 30 days, $5 million and $1 million. Only the Board Risk Committee can.
