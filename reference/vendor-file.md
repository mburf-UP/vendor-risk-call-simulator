# Fernhook: what the bank has on file

Fernhook is invented. This is what Fernhook sent the bank, and Maree read all of it before the call. Apart from this, she knows what `bank.md` records about Fernhook and what she is told on the call.

## The one-page summary Fernhook sent

> **Hardship Drafts, by Fernhook**
>
> Fernhook is a two-person AI automation studio in Brisbane. Hardship Drafts reads each hardship email as it arrives in your team's shared mailbox and writes a draft reply. One of your officers edits the draft and sends it. Nothing goes to a member without a person at the bank sending it.
>
> Bank-grade security. Runs entirely in Australia. Built by our founders, with trusted specialist contractors as needed. We can be live within three weeks.

## What Maree noticed when she read it

- The form says "Not retained". Clause 7.2 says up to 30 days.
- The summary mentions contractors. The form lists two founders and no names.
- Clause 14.1 lets Fernhook use subcontractors and gives no list.
- Item 5 is blank.
- "As soon as reasonably practicable" is not 24 hours, and 90 days is not 30.
- "PI: yes" is not an amount.
